SO-ARM101 推物体 · HIL-SERL 真机在线强化学习 SO-ARM101 Object Pushing · HIL-SERL Real-Robot Online RL

个人项目 · 独立完成全流程 · LeRobot / HIL-SERL / SAC / SO-ARM101 Personal project · end-to-end solo · LeRobot / HIL-SERL / SAC / SO-ARM101
背景Context
模仿学习(ACT / SmolVLA)的瓶颈很明确:示教要几十条,而策略上限就是示教水平、做错了不会自己纠。HIL-SERL 换了思路 —— 只用约 10 条示教起手,之后让机械臂自己跑,推歪了人接管一下,接管数据再喂回强化学习,策略越跑越不需要人管。这个项目正是我在 SmolVLA 卡片里写的"引入在线 RL"改进方向的落地。 Imitation learning (ACT / SmolVLA) has a clear ceiling: it needs dozens of demonstrations, the policy can only match demonstration quality, and it cannot correct its own mistakes. HIL-SERL inverts this — start from roughly 10 demonstrations, then let the arm run itself, with a human taking over when it goes wrong; those takeover samples feed back into RL until intervention is barely needed. This project realizes the "online RL" direction I listed as future work on the SmolVLA card.
我的职责My role
独立负责全流程:把 HIL-SERL 移植适配到本机 LeRobot 0.6.1(含 10 处本地补丁)、实测标定本机参数三件套(起始姿势 / EE 围栏 / 图像裁剪框)、录制 12 条示教、跑 learner–actor 双进程在线训练、真机部署与 20 次换位测试,并完整归档踩坑档案。 Owned everything: ported and adapted HIL-SERL to my local LeRobot 0.6.1 (10 local patches), measured the three machine-specific calibrations (home pose / end-effector bounds / image crop box), recorded 12 demonstrations, ran the two-process learner–actor online training, deployed to hardware, ran 20 repositioned trials, and documented the full troubleshooting archive.
技术方案Approach
在线 SAC,learner(策略服务器 + 训练)与 actor(真机执行 + 采集)双进程经 gRPC 通信:learner 持续把最新参数推给 actor,actor 把 transition 回传入池。人在环干预的数据同样进池,每集比上一集少干预。任务设计上刻意选"推物体进胶带区"而非抓取-放置 —— 时程短(1~2 秒一回合,适合回合制在线采样)、奖励明确(进没进,人按一下键即为 1)、探索空间小,这三条直接决定了能否在几十分钟内出效果。又因为推物体全程不用夹爪,把动作空间从 4 维裁到 3 维,少学一个自由度换取更快收敛。 Online SAC with a two-process learner (policy server + training) and actor (hardware rollout + collection) talking over gRPC: the learner streams fresh parameters to the actor, the actor returns transitions to the buffer. Human-takeover samples enter the same buffer, and intervention drops episode over episode. I deliberately chose "push the object into a taped zone" over pick-and-place — short horizon (1–2 s per episode, well suited to episodic online sampling), unambiguous reward (a single keypress marks success), and a small exploration space; those three choices are what make results reachable in tens of minutes. Since pushing never uses the gripper, I cut the action space from 4-D to 3-D, trading one degree of freedom for faster convergence.
量化结果Results
仅 12 条示教(2,087 帧)+ 在线自采 · 21,000 步约 45 分钟真机训练即达到较好效果(参考教程同路线首次约 2 小时)· 真机 20 次测试、每次更换起始位置,20/20 全部成功 ✅ Just 12 demonstrations (2,087 frames) plus online self-collection · 21,000 optimization steps in ~45 min of real-robot training to reach solid performance (the reference tutorial's first run on the same route took ~2 h) · 20 hardware trials, each from a different start position — 20/20 succeeded ✅
工程踩坑Gotchas
12 集里有 8 集奖励全程为 0 —— 原因是那些集跑满了 20 秒时限自动结束,时限到了不算成功、不会有任何标注,推到位必须马上按键;actor 崩了 learner 不会知道,learner 往队列推参数不检查有没有人收,会继续"对着空气"训练、步数照涨,所以停的时候要先停 actor 再停 learner;某次 actor 直接退出,线索是只有夹爪(id=6)串口写失败、前 5 个舵机都正常 —— 定位到那颗舵机接触不良,重插线即可。 8 of the 12 episodes had all-zero reward — they had run out the 20 s time limit, and hitting the limit is not success and produces no label at all, so the success key must be pressed the moment the object lands. The learner never notices when the actor dies: it pushes parameters to a queue without checking for a consumer, so it keeps "training against thin air" while the step count climbs — stop the actor first, then the learner. One actor crash was traced by noticing only the gripper (id=6) failed its serial write while the other five servos were fine, pointing at a loose connector on that one servo.
局限Limitations
奖励判定用的是人工按键而非奖励分类器 —— 试过分类器但分类不稳定,最终关闭改回人工,这也与参考路线的结论一致。另外训练指标只往 wandb 送,配置里没开 wandb 时终端和日志里一个字都看不到,漏开就补不上。 Success is judged by a human keypress rather than a learned reward classifier — I tried the classifier but its predictions were unstable, so I disabled it and reverted to manual keying, matching the reference route's own conclusion. Also, training metrics are sent only to wandb: with wandb disabled in the config, nothing appears in the terminal or logs, and a missed run cannot be recovered after the fact.

SO-ARM101 机械臂抓取-放置 · SmolVLA 视觉-语言-动作模型微调 SO-ARM101 Pick-and-Place · SmolVLA Vision-Language-Action Fine-Tuning

个人项目 · 独立完成全流程 · LeRobot / SmolVLA / PyTorch / SO-ARM101 Personal project · end-to-end solo · LeRobot / SmolVLA / PyTorch / SO-ARM101
背景Context
在完成 ACT 模仿学习闭环之后,进一步探索视觉-语言-动作模型(VLA)路线:用大规模预训练的 VLA 权重在低成本机械臂上做小样本微调,验证"预训练先验能否带来动作质量增益",并与 ACT 基线做同任务对比。 After closing the ACT imitation-learning loop, I explored the vision-language-action (VLA) route: few-shot fine-tuning of large pretrained VLA weights on a low-cost arm, to test whether pretrained priors improve action quality — benchmarked against the ACT baseline on the same task.
我的职责My role
独立负责全流程:带语言指令的示教采集(v1 40 条 + 补录 v2 15 条,合并为 v3 共 55 条 / 15,625 帧)、SmolVLA 微调、真机部署,以及一次完整的失败归因与数据驱动修复闭环。 Owned everything: teleop collection with language instructions (v1 40 episodes + a 15-episode v2 top-up, merged into v3 — 55 episodes / 15,625 frames), SmolVLA fine-tuning, hardware deployment, and one complete failure-diagnosis-to-data-fix loop.
技术方案Approach
LeRobot 0.6.1 + SmolVLA(450M 参数,冻结 VLM 骨干、仅训练约 100M 参数的动作专家,Flow Matching 动作头),微调 20,000 步。输入为单目 RGB 1080p@25FPS + 6 维关节状态 + 语言指令。冻结视觉编码器后 11.5GB 显存可跑 batch=8,实测约 3.8 step/s。单相机数据通过 rename_map 映射并补两个空相机位以对齐模型的多相机输入约定。 LeRobot 0.6.1 + SmolVLA (450M params — VLM backbone frozen, only the ~100M-parameter action expert trained, flow-matching action head), fine-tuned for 20,000 steps. Inputs: monocular RGB 1080p@25FPS, 6-D joint state, and a language instruction. With the vision encoder frozen, 11.5 GB VRAM fits batch=8 at ~3.8 step/s. The single-camera dataset is mapped via rename_map with two empty camera slots padded to satisfy the model's multi-camera input convention.
量化结果Results
真机稳定完成抓取-放置 ✅ · 55 条示教 / 15,625 帧 · 夹取动作质量优于 ACT 基线(贴住再夹更稳定),验证了同数据量下 VLA 预训练先验的动作质量增益 Reliable pick-and-place on hardware ✅ · 55 demonstrations / 15,625 frames · better grasp quality than the ACT baseline (contacts before closing, so it holds more reliably), confirming a pretrained-prior advantage at equal data volume
失败归因Debugging
v1 模型部署时稳定地"在纸巾卷上方约 1cm 处夹紧",夹爪闭合却够不到物体。关键一步是把物体垫高 1cm 的对照实验 —— 行为完全不变,说明模型并没有在看物体高度、而是在照轨迹模板走,由此排除"看到了但算错高度"的假设。回看训练数据后定位根因:遥操作时夹取时机偏早,演示本身就是悬空夹的,而行为克隆学到的正是演示的平均行为。修复方式是数据驱动的:补录 15 条"夹爪贴住后闭合、停 1 秒再提起"的示教并增加物体位置变体,重新微调后部署成功。 The v1 policy consistently closed its gripper ~1 cm above the tissue roll — gripping air. The decisive step was a controlled experiment raising the object by 1 cm: behavior did not change at all, showing the policy was not reading object height but replaying a trajectory template, which ruled out "sees it but miscalculates height." Reviewing the training data found the root cause: teleop closed the gripper too early, so the demonstrations themselves grasped at empty air — and behavior cloning learns the average demonstrated behavior. The fix was data-driven: 15 additional episodes where the gripper contacts the roll, closes, holds one second, then lifts, plus more position variants; after re-fine-tuning, deployment succeeded.
工程踩坑Gotchas
语言指令是 VLA 的命脉:LeRobot 录制默认不写 tasks.parquet 的任务描述,必须补齐,且训练与部署的口令要一字不差。此外部署相机分辨率须与训练一致(1920×1080),1080p 下必须指定 MJPG 编码(否则回退 YUYV 仅 5 FPS)—— 与 ACT 项目同款教训。 The language instruction is the lifeline of a VLA: LeRobot's recorder does not write the task description into tasks.parquet by default, so it must be backfilled — and the prompt must match verbatim between training and deployment. Deployment camera resolution must also match training (1920×1080), and at 1080p the camera must be forced to MJPG (otherwise YUYV caps it at 5 FPS) — the same lessons as the ACT project.
局限Limitations
泛化能力受限于数据多样性与单目视角:补录的位置变体有限,对全新位置泛化一般;仅一路 front 相机使模型缺少多视角深度信息。其中"引入在线 RL"这条改进方向已在后续的 HIL-SERL 项目中落地(见上一张卡片),剩余方向为多相机采集与更多位置变体。 Generalization is bounded by data diversity and the monocular viewpoint: the added position variants are limited, so novel placements generalize only moderately, and a single front camera leaves the model without multi-view depth cues. Of the follow-ups I identified, "move to online RL" has since been realized in the HIL-SERL project above; multi-camera collection and more position variants remain.

Domain randomization over friction, link mass, and joint parameters, plus periodic external pushes.
